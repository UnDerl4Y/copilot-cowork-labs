# Group Build Execution Guide

## Stage 1 - Understand

Read the assigned use-case requirements.

## Stage 2 - Inspect

Review the completed skill, connector, plugin and agent.

## Stage 3 - Integrate

Connect the components and verify their configuration.

## Stage 4 - Process

Execute the solution against the unseen dataset.

## Stage 5 - Validate

Check data accuracy, business rules, tool responses and outputs.

## Stage 6 - Prepare

Organize the demonstration and technical evidence.

## Trainer support

Record the issue, affected component, attempted resolution
and assistance required.
