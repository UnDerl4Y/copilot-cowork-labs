# Group Build Challenge Brief

## Business challenge

Complete the assigned enterprise use case end to end.

## Starting point

Participants must reuse the artifacts developed during the three guided labs.

## Challenge constraints

- Use the assigned business requirements.
- Use the completed custom skill.
- Use the completed MCP connector.
- Use the packaged plugin.
- Reuse the MCP server in Copilot Studio.
- Process the supplied unseen dataset.
- Do not invent missing business data.
- Surface validation failures and missing evidence.
- Require approval for governed write operations.

## Success

The solution produces the required business deliverable
and satisfies its use-case acceptance criteria.
