# Business Requirements

## Business problem

Incident timelines and root-cause evidence are difficult to assemble across ticket and change systems.

## Business owner

Chief Information Officer

## Business objective

Describe the expected business outcome.

## Users and stakeholders

Identify the primary users and reviewers.

## Inputs

Document required data sources and fields.

## Processing requirements

Document the required business workflow.

## Constraints

Document policy, security and approval constraints.

## Expected output

Word post-mortem identifying the triggering change and a governed problem ticket.
